Talking with my family about this
I have an appointment next Tuesday and would like to have some questions for my doctor, but am not sure what good questions are.

Here are 6 questions families often find useful at this stage:

  1. What stage are we at, and what should I expect over the next 6–12 months?
  2. Which current symptoms might be treatable vs. progression?
  3. What medications are we on, and what side effects should I watch for?
  4. When should I call you vs. go to the ER?
  5. Are there local services or trials you'd recommend?
  6. How do we plan for the next big transition (driving, finances, care)?

These are general starting questions. Want me to tailor them based on the stage or any specific symptoms?
